Community Flash-Flood Alert System

Early, area-specific flood warnings for residents along the Mekong in Kratie, Cambodia.

Course project — proposal stage (v1)

The problem

Kratie sits directly on the Mekong River and floods most rainy seasons. Today, warnings reach people through television, Facebook, or commune loudspeakers — often too general, too slow, or missed at night. Families are left with too little time to move to higher ground before the water arrives.

What we're building

A simple web application backed by Telegram alerts. It monitors the Mekong's water level near Kratie, shows the current flood risk, and warns subscribed residents early, with safety information and emergency contacts. A commune officer can review the readings and confirm or send an alert.

How it works

  1. Fetch river-discharge and rainfall data for Kratie several times a day (Open-Meteo, free).
  2. Compare it to danger thresholds and mark the risk as Normal, Caution, or Danger.
  3. Show the current risk, safety information, and contacts on the website.
  4. When the risk becomes Danger, a Telegram bot alerts everyone subscribed for that area.

Built with

A web app, the free Open-Meteo flood and rainfall data, a Telegram bot, and a relational database — using only free and open data. Danger levels are modelled on the Mekong River Commission's caution level for Kratie (22.0 m).
